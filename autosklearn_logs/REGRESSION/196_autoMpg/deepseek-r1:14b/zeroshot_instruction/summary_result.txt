 The AutoML process utilized SMAC (Sequential Model-Based Optimization) to optimize a regression task. The system employed a combination of Local Search and Random Search strategies to explore configurations, aiming to maximize efficiency in identifying the optimal machine learning pipeline. Key steps included evaluating 35 configurations, with local searches taking varying steps and examining numerous configurations to compute the acquisition function. Gaussian Process regressor was selected as the final model due to its superior performance under the given constraints. The process efficiently utilized computational resources, adhering to time limits while ensuring thorough exploration of potential solutions.
