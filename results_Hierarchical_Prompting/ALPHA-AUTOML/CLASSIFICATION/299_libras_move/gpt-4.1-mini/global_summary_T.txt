###### Component-Level Explanation

- **IMPUTER:** Handles any missing data. Although the dataset reports no missing values, including an imputer ensures robustness for potential edge cases.  
- **ENCODERS:** Not active on numeric-only data, appears in pipeline but serves no effect given dataset characteristics.  
- **FEATURE_SCALER (StandardScaler / MaxAbsScaler):** Scales numeric features to normalize their ranges; particularly relevant for coordinate data to ensure model convergence and comparability. MaxAbsScaler variant used for scaling while preserving sparsity and non-negativity.  
- **FEATURE_SELECTOR (GenericUnivariateSelect):** Selects relevant features based on statistical tests, reducing dimensionality from 90 features. Critical given high feature count to improve model efficiency.  
- **CLASSIFIER (includes GaussianNB, KNeighborsClassifier, GradientBoostingClassifier):** Models evaluated. GradientBoostingClassifier replaced generic classifier achieving improved performance.

###### Outcome Explanation

- Pipeline marked as **success**, because it achieved a concrete evaluation score (0.0694), showing active training and validation completed.  
- The addition of feature scaling and selection combined with advanced classifiers such as Gradient Boosting improved predictive effect, evident from scoring versus base pipelines.  
- Simpler pipelines (e.g., only IMPUTER + CLASSIFIER) show higher scores, confirming importance of scaling and selection.

# Summary

The AutoML process started with a numeric-only dataset containing 90 coordinate features with no missing or useless data. The pipeline search iteratively constructed increasingly complex pipelines starting from a simple base (S), adding components stepwise—IMPUTER, FEATURE_SCALER (and variants), FEATURE_SELECTOR, and multiple classifiers. Multiple simulations ran in parallel, assessing pipeline effectiveness via scores. Successful pipelines notably combined MaxAbsScaler with GenericUnivariateSelect and GradientBoostingClassifier, achieving the best score ~0.0694. The process explored various classifiers and pipeline structures, with some simulations failing or remaining in creation states. Ultimately, the search was forcibly terminated on timeout, having discovered 153 candidate pipelines. This detailed systematic search demonstrates the AutoML's incremental and evaluative approach to pipeline optimization grounded in dataset characteristics exposed during profiling.
